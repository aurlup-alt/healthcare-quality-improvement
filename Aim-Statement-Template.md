- Could improvement differ across population groups?

- What balancing measure could identify unintended consequences?

## 7. Check the Aim Statement
Before starting, confirm that the aim is:
- [ ] Specific
- [ ] Measurable
- [ ] Relevant to patients and service users
- [ ] Realistic for the available resources
- [ ] Time-bound
- [ ] Supported by a defined measurement plan

## Example
"By 31 March 2027, increase the proportion of eligible patients receiving documented blood pressure measurement at the selected primary healthcare facility from a verified baseline of 60% to at least 80%, without creating unreasonable delays in patient care."

*This example is illustrative. Verify the baseline, feasibility, and target locally before adopting it.*

## Next Steps
1. Validate the problem with relevant staff and, where appropriate, patients.
2. Confirm baseline data and agree on a feasible target.
3. Identify change ideas.
4. Use PDSA cycles to test changes and learn from the results.
5. Review progress regularly and revise the approach when needed.
