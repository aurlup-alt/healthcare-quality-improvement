## Responsible Use
- Define each measure consistently before collecting data.
- Use reliable, relevant data and document changes to definitions.
- Protect patient confidentiality and follow local data protection requirements.
- Do not upload identifiable patient data or confidential records to a public repository.
- Interpret results in context; improvement measures support learning and do not by themselves prove causation.
