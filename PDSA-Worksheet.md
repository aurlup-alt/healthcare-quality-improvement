### Measures
- **Outcome measure:** What result matters to patients or the system?
- **Process measure:** Was the planned change carried out?
- **Balancing measure:** Could the change cause unintended problems elsewhere?

For each measure, define the numerator, denominator (if applicable), data source, collection frequency, and responsible person.

## Repeat the Cycle

Use the learning from this cycle to plan the next PDSA cycle. Document each cycle separately so the team can track changes, results, and learning over time.

## Responsible Use

- Protect patient confidentiality; do not enter identifiable patient information in this worksheet or a public repository.
- Follow local clinical policies, ethical requirements, and data protection rules.
- Use appropriate baseline data and repeated measurements where feasible.
- Do not assume that an observed change was caused by the intervention without considering other explanations.
- Involve patients and relevant staff where appropriate.
