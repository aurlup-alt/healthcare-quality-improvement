## 5. Interpretation

- What pattern or trend do you observe?
- Is the measure improving, worsening, or remaining stable?
- Did any unusual changes occur?
- What changes were introduced during the period?
- What will the team test or do next?

## 6. Important Considerations

- Use consistent definitions and data collection methods over time.
- Display data in chronological order.
- Interpret results in the context of the clinical setting and other relevant measures.
- Avoid drawing strong conclusions from a small number of data points.
- Protect patient confidentiality; do not include identifiable patient information.
